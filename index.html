Standup Timer for Google Docs™

Run your daily standup inside a Google Doc with a per-person timebox. Everyone who opens the sidebar sees the same speaker and the same countdown.

Getting started

  1. Open your standup doc → Extensions → Standup Timer → Open timer.
  2. Open Participants, click Import from headings to pull names from your headings (H2 by default; H1–H3 selectable), or type Name, seconds per line, then click Save.
  3. Press Start. Use Next / Prev or the ← → keys to change speakers, Space to pause, and +30s to extend.
  4. When you are done, Write log appends a summary table to the end of the doc.

The sidebar follows your browser language (English or Korean).
