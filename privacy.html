Privacy Policy

Standup Timer for Google Docs™ · Effective October 14, 2026

Standup Timer is a Google Docs™ add-on that runs entirely on Google Apps Script. The developer operates no servers and does not sell or share any personal data. Your document content is never sent to the developer.

What the add-on accesses

  • The current document only (documents.currentonly): it reads heading text to import participant names (for an @-mention chip without a display name, only the part of the email address before “@” is used), moves your cursor to the current speaker's heading if you turn on “Move my cursor to the speaker”, and, only when you click “Write log”, appends a summary table with participant names and times to the end of the document, where it becomes ordinary document content.
  • Sidebar and menu (script.container.ui): to show the timer sidebar inside Google Docs.

Where data is stored

  • Participant names, time limits, and timer state are saved as properties of the document itself (Google Apps Script document properties and document cache) so every editor of that document sees the same timer. They stay with that document and are removed when the document is deleted or when you save an empty participant list. “Reset” clears only the timer round, not the names, and uninstalling the add-on does not delete them, so save an empty list first if you want them gone.
  • Your personal sidebar settings (sound, follow cursor, heading level, default seconds) are kept in your browser's local storage.

Diagnostics

  • Your account's language setting is read only to choose the menu language.
  • Google Apps Script may show the developer anonymous execution error reports (error messages from the add-on's code, no document content) and install counts from Google Workspace Marketplace. They are used only to fix bugs.

What we do not do

  • Apart from the diagnostics above, no data is sent to the developer or to any third party. No analytics, advertising, or tracking.
  • No access to other documents, your Drive, email, or contacts.

Standup Timer's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
